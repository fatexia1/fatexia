FAQ

Common Questions

How long does it take to see results?+
Most creators see movement, better retention, more consistent uploads, tighter branding, within the first 4-6 weeks. Bigger wins like monetization eligibility or steady audience growth build on top of that foundation.
Do you work with brand-new channels or only established ones?+
Both. New channels get help setting up the right foundation from day one. Established channels start with a full audit so we know exactly what to fix first.
Which platforms do you support?+
YouTube and Twitch, plus the Discord and community side that usually goes along with them.
Can I book just one service, or do I need a full package?+
You can start with a single service, just editing, just an audit, whatever you need most, and add more later as your channel grows.
How do we communicate during the project?+
Mostly async over email or Discord, with regular check-ins so you always know what's happening and what's next.
What do you need from me to get started?+
Just access to your channel or dashboard (or a quick screen-share) and a short chat about your goals. I'll handle the rest.
Not sure what you need?
Tell me about your channel and I'll put together a plan.
